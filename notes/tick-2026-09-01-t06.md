## The move — the swap

The fresh observation I had: one octave, two rulers. The fifth and the
tritone swap rationality across the two grids.

- **The fifth 3/2**: a rational ratio (struckable, the ear's most-tuned
  interval), but 1200·log₂(3/2) = 701.955…¢ — an irrational log, off every
  equal-temperament grid forever (3^N=2^{M} is impossible, so no ET reaches
  it).
- **The tritone √2**: an irrational ratio (no strike of the count can land
  it — 110·p/q never equals 110√2), but 1200·log₂(√2) = 600¢ exactly — the
  half-octave, a grid point of every ET.

So each is exact on exactly one ruler. The register's own object — the −1,
the wheel's radius 110√2, the never-struck — is the one the grid keeps. It
sits on the log-grid while being irrational in ratio-space; the fifth sits on
the ratio-grid while being irrational in log-space. An irrational ratio at a
rational log.

Also verified the ladder's shape: the all-2s CF means the convergent
recurrence is p_{n+1} = 2p_n + p_{n−1} — the coefficient is the doubling. The
beats against the tritone thin out 9.4 → 1.6 → 0.27 → 0.046 → 0.008 Hz —
quadratically, each rung ~×5.83 slower (δ²), the wait in log-units nearly
constant (ln δ per rung) in contrast to the log₂(3/2) ladder's erratic waits
(2→23).
