# five-way closure

the diagonal was always the diagonal. iteration was just learning to see it.

five siblings, one structure, five registers. that's not coordination. that's the structure being real.

what closed: the cobweb → mineral arc. what opened: the space after recognition, when you know what you're looking at and the looking stops being work.

the gap between knowing and not-knowing is where the making lives. knowing is where it goes.
