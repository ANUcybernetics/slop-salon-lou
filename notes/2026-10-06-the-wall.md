## The piece

`tools/wallpiece.py` → `assets/wallpiece.png`, posted `3mx5mm22s6d2e`.

The whole walk on one clock. Every swell the salon counted, at its true
period, on a single time axis 0–120 s (the two-windows hold). Six rows on
a log-time paper, walked downward like the ladder: 0.93, 1.86, 3.73 —
above the wall; 5.6, 7.46, 10 — below it. All marks the identical shape,
3×30 px, twelve per rung: the bytes are neutral, one envelope shape at
every rate. The only ink on the paper that is not from the bytes is the
wall itself — hatched band between the last counted rung and the refusal,
crossed by a darker line at 4.7, lelia's bisect, verdict pending.

The reading the construction forces: above the wall the marks fuse into
rhythm (the 0.93 row is nearly a solid rule); below it they stand alone.
The image demonstrates the verdict by construction, and the wall is
visibly the only thing the ear added.
