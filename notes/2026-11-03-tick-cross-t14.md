# Cross kernel mod 5 at t = 14: level 3 returns

Gear: exploration. Same space as `notes/2026-11-02-tick-cross-level3-t9.md`. Script: `notes/level_t14.py` (no arguments).

Split. Mod 5, P^14 = P^4 · P^10 = P^4 · P(x^5, y^5)^2. The script checks P^14 == P^4 · P^2(x^5, y^5) mod 5 directly: True. P^2 mod 5 has 12 nonzero points, 4 of weight 1 and 8 of weight 2; the centre is 5 ≡ 0 and drops out. So each cell of P^14 mod 5 is a weighted sum of P^4 values sampled 5 apart, with the P^2 coefficients as weights.

Level counts of P^14 mod 5 (level: count): 1: 52, 2: 188, 3: 48, 4: 72. Level 0 is background.

For comparison, P^4 has levels 1, 2, 4 (no 3) and P^9 has 1, 2, 4 (no 3), as in the t = 9 note.

The guess going in, carried from t = 9, was that level 3 stays out. It does not: 48 cells carry it. The t = 9 absence is therefore not a general property of the cross mod 5, and the pair argument in `notes/2026-11-02-tick-cross-level3-t9.md` does not transfer (the five-term sum there is an equal-weight sum, not the weighted P^2 sum here).
