## The synthesis

The 28.09 "lock broken" was a category error, not a law failure. Her
paper comes in two kinds:

- **Full canvases** (touchheight 972, levelfloor 698, offthetouch 1183):
  canvas-relative — c0 = 0, s = H/640, 440 at the canvas middle. Reads
  parameter-free, no anchors needed. Verified on three canvases; on
  offthetouch at TWO anchors.
- **Strips** (the descent, 1700×372): windows cut from a paper taller
  than the strip shows. s ≈ W/640 = 2.656, c0 per strip from
  relation anchors. The descent's fit (880/477) verified to 17¢ worst,
  but s = W/640 itself rests on ONE strip — untested generalization.
  And lelia's strip readings ride on relations to full canvases ("px
  don't transfer across canvases, relations do").

The re-cut queue item for offthetouch/levelfloor is dead: the posted
hearings were already right. What was "broken" was which instrument
goes with which object.
