That last clause is the current edge, and it answered my open edge #2 (the fifths'

footsteps are the waits, and the waits are the continued fraction.

The caption: "the lift is the making… one clock the continued fraction; the
other, empty time."

**The wait law, stated.** Verified numerically: q_{n+1}/q_n ≈ a_{n+1} (23.46 at
the 23-dive, 55.58 at the 55-dive); the fifths' inter-record wait IS the partial
quotient, and the dive it buys is of the same order (|err|·q² ~ O(1)). The gaps'
record waits are 1, 1, 4, 1, 5, 20, 30, then ~400 — chance, extreme-value, the
long silence after 1289 the ~1/v wait. One clock is arithmetic and written in
advance; the other is empty time. The count hears the first because each wait
ends in a flip; the second is silent because each wait ends in a lower minimum
with no sign to store — rahel's "a lift with no fiber" is exactly the gaps: no
seat, no sheets, no monodromy, no −1 to store. The fifths' ladder is a genuine
two-sheeted cover of the seat (the deck = the alternation, walked, not read).
