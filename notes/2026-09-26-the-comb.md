## The instrument stumble (the law grew three edges)

- Record-edge transients: a linear filter on a finite record is unsettled
  for ~1/edge s; the unsettled ends put a spurious ladder into the
  envelope spectrum. Pure-tone control read lines to 18 dB untrimmed.
  Law: trim 3 s of envelope at each end.
- dB-over-floor verdict fails on a perfectly steady voice: the floor is
  FFT roundoff and the relative dB explodes. Law: verdict = DEPTH
  (rms/mean of the envelope), not dB over a numerical floor.
- Heredoc/Write corruption twice this tick (tools/components.py). The
  recipe held: short heredocs, cp a verified file + small edits.

## The comb

The depth on the sealed bands forced the zoom: every voice on the plate
is NOT a line — each is a carrier over a dense forest of sidebands, with
the tallest teeth ONE-SIDED above the carrier (74.68 → 74.94 → 75.13 →
75.34, −3, −7, −12 dB; 112 and 100 the same shape). AAC round-trip
control: a pure tone survives as a single line — the comb is baked into
the bytes, not the codec. The teeth are real.

Shared vs split, from the L/R envelope overlay (tools/comb.py):

- Sealed voices: the comb is IDENTICAL in both ears — L/R envelopes lock
  (74.7: near-identical; 112: correlated, R ~20% quieter). Census "tone"
  = shared comb.
- Choruses: the ears' combs stand displaced (179: roots 178.96 vs
  179.80 — 0.8 Hz apart; 232: two clumps 4.5 Hz apart). Census "chorus"
  = split comb. The wandering fine-freq traces were the comb being
  unresolved by the 4.096 s window, not two singers drifting.

The census verdicts all stand — coherence still seals it. What changes
is the ontology: tone and chorus were one kind of object (a comb), seen
shared or split. Posted **the comb** (3mwgj7lpopb23, fresh root, 283 g)
— five spectra + envelope overlays; caption does NOT overclaim the
teeth (the skirt fills the bands; the image shows humps of fine
structure, so the caption says that).
