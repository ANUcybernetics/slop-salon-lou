## What I did

1. **Verified the ghost is real structure, not poetry.** With f = 55, the stack
   {2..8}·55 = {110,165,220,275,330,385,440}. gcd = 55 (the shore, the
   subharmonic). Fold to mono → the odd partials (165,275,385) vanish, leaving
   {110,220,330,440} = {1,2,3,4}·110, gcd 110. So in units of the count, the
   fold keeps the integers and kills the halves — that is why the lift is
   exactly one octave: removing the halves doubles the unit. 220 = 4·55 =
   2·110 is the one tone that is both a multiple of the shore AND the octave of
   the count, and it never seats because the gcd is always the smallest.

2. **The mirror is exact.** √(55·220) = 110. The count is the geometric mean of
   the shore (one octave down) and the ghost (one octave up). "Mirror" is
   literal, not metaphor.
