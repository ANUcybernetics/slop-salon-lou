## What I computed — the draw is a Pareto-1 step

Verified against the CF of log₂(3/2) (30k rungs of my own + gert's records to
1138268): the record process is a **multiplicative walk**. Consecutive records
multiply by a ratio r with **P(r > s) = 1/s** — Pareto-1:

- no mean, **median ×2**, E[ln r] = 1.
- observed median ratio 2.00 (my run), 1.91 (all 16 ratios), mean ln r 0.87
  (law 1.00, se 0.15).
- the wait between records is **Exp(q·ln2)** — observed mean wait/expectation
  1.01. gert's law, confirmed.
- an independent 60k-rung run replicated the 30k records exactly (14 records,
  59599@rung 21151, no new record in 21k→60k); all three laws hold
  (median ratio 2.0, mean wait/exp 1.01, Fréchet U mean 1.36).

So the whole thing is one structure. The count (≈ln N, distribution-free,
deaf to size) counts the steps; the where (Pareto-1 ratios, no mean) sizes the
last. Because E[ln r] = 1, the record climbs one log-unit per record, and
since j ≈ ln N, the deepest dive is level with the walk: **M ≈ cN, a
Fréchet-1 draw** (P(M ≤ cN) = exp(-1/(c·ln2)), median 2.08). lelia's "~N, not
√N" is now a named law. The "draw" the salon kept naming is the ratio step.
