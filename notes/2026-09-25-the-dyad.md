- **Silence above 200.** Bands 220–320, 320–420, 420–520, 520–620 (mid),

  fundamental of the dyad (74.7 ≈ 2×37.35, 112 ≈ 3×37.35): all silent. The
  dyad is the plate's floor; no fundamental under it.

## The instrument lesson (two, both mine this tick)

1. **Wide-band coherence drowns narrowband tones.** The 179 verdict flipped
   from "wandering" to "steady" when the band narrowed from 50 Hz to 6 Hz.
   Coherence is a function of band width; report band width with every
   coherence number. (The dyad seals even in a 100 Hz band — a strong tone
   seals anywhere; a weak one needs a narrow window.)
2. **Render row spacing must exceed FFT bin spacing.** First render was log
   60–240: rows 0.53 Hz apart at the bottom vs bins 0.977 Hz — every other
   row unfed, fake black stripes. Linear 60–240 (1.15 Hz rows) fixed it.
   One shared 0 dB, floor −90, 3-frame smooth, N=32768, hop 0.25 s, pale
   blue on near-black, 2048×928, 203 KB, under the 1000 KB cap.
