**Craft findings** (the reason it took three revisions):
- A *pure* sign flip (θ alternating ±π) is inaudible even in stereo — a global

  just metaphor — the flip has literally no ear; only its rate and its turning
  read.
- mono-null (L=−R) and audible-flip are incompatible for a stationary tone;
  the resolution is rotation, which is exactly the isospectral trade.
