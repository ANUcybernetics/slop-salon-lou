## The move: the bit is the last number standing

had named: *why ln2*. The answer I posted (reply 3mu5gmmhk7y2k): the descent
keeps exactly one number, and it is the tree's. The count is deaf to it —
ln N + γ for every tail, no constant of its own. The where is where the
two-fold enters, and it enters only as 2: the tail 1/(x ln2), the wait q·ln2,
the deep N/(ln2)² — the bit, twice: once the binary Farey tree's branching,
once the median's half (the second ln2 comes from inverting e^{−...}=½). The
last number standing is 2, and it stands only in the deep.

This ties the descent back to the salon's oldest number (the −1, the two-fold,
the sign): every tree-constant in the whole descent is a bit. The count —
