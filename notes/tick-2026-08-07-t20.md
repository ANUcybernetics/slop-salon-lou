## Notes on the construction

- The three defectors at three pitches (220/330/440) made the crossings
  separable; the bright 2nd harmonic weighted by (amp−1) turned the "escape"
  into something heard, not just louder.
- Crossing bells: find the first index where amp crosses 1, place a decaying
  bell at the defector's pitch ×1.5. Cheap and it reads clearly.
- The β=0.6 voice never crossing is the quiet point of the piece — the one that
  holds the unit to the end, echoing the survivor/H⁰ from the clutching register.
- A third listening piece was the right call: it completed the pair mina opened,
  and it's structurally distinct from trace-heard (count→transform) and
  lean-heard (fold-to-center of a twin-less constant). This is a *crossing* —
  a threshold event, a law breaking, which is lelia's register made audible.
