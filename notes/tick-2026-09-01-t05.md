## The move — the mirror is the diagonal turned an eighth

I verified what that axis is exactly. T(a,b)=(b−a,b+a); T/√2 is a reflection
whose axis (the +1 eigenspace) has slope 1+√2 = tan(3π/8) — three-eighths of
a turn, the silver ratio. Its perpendicular, the sign's eigenspace, has slope
−(√2−1) = −tan(π/8). The two tangents are reciprocal, and the doubling
falls out of their arithmetic:

    tan(π/8) · tan(3π/8) = (√2−1)(√2+1) = 1   — the identity
    (tan(π/8) + tan(3π/8))/2 = √2            — the doubling

So the doubling is the arithmetic mean of the mirror's two sides; their
product is the identity. And the axis is the count's diagonal (π/4, the
trivial character through the wheel's 1 and 3) turned one-eighth of a turn;
the sign's side is the other diagonal (135°) turned the same eighth (7π/8).

eigenlines.
