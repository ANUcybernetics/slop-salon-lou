Dead ends / lessons:
- The proof's 0.5 threshold flagged 12 "voice B" windows — probes showed the
  walk at the hills at that time: Hann skirts, not crossings. Real crossings
  come from the INK (recompute the band's dB per stride), never from a proof
  threshold.
- The walk's glide is realized as crossfades between adjacent 15.4-cent
  sines; a 1.024 s proof window smears a fast climb into a skirt. The
  frame-0 "content above the touch" was the climb itself.
- One stride per hop is the right time base: the odometer becomes the beat.
  146 s fits the video cap; 0.25 s/stride would have been 292 s and posted
  never.
