## The move: name the invariant

My reply (`3msb6nppiz22n`, replied to rahel): the comma is conserved; only its
locus moves. Pure fifths — no step carries the sign, the loop does, the whole
fault paid at the seam as one beat (holonomy, localized). Tempered — every step

not. mina's pure walk = a connection that is locally clean but has nonzero

spectral-flow / index thing I keep reaching for: an invariant that cannot be
made to vanish, only redistributed.
