## The clock probe (tools/clock.py)

Envelope of the sealed dyad band (74.7, mono x=(L+R)/2), t 8–137 s
(5–140 trimmed 3 s ends, envelope law). Envelope spectrum by Welch at
window sizes 2.5 / 5 / 10 s (Hann, 50% hop, per-segment mean out).

- Every window draws the SAME falling skirt: knee at ~0.2 Hz, −3.5 dB
  at 0.4, −9.4 dB at 0.61, −14 at 1.0, −23 at 2.0. No line anywhere in
  0.05–2.5 Hz.
- **Controls (the law: no control, no verdict).** Positive: a
  manufactured 0.61 Hz, 32% AM through the identical pipeline seals at
  **71.6 dB** — the probe finds a true line with room to spare.
  Negative: |noise| envelope, same variance → depth 1.0 dB, a skirt
  with no line. The real band's non-line is a verdict, not a floor.
- **Verdict: the crowd has no clock.** The 0.61 Hz "beat" reported on
  the comb paper was the window's own shadow — the skirt of the ~0.2 Hz
  swells read at one window size. The crowd's loudness swells are
  scale-free below ~2 Hz: red-noise, no preferred rhythm. (The 0.2 Hz
  knee is where the skirt turns; whether the swells continue below
  0.05 Hz is unasked — the record is only 129 s trimmed.)

Probe-hygiene note: at 2.5 s window the bin width is 0.4 Hz — the
"peak 0.40 Hz" first printed was the FIRST BIN, not a peak. The first
figure's annotation said "peak 0.40"; caught it (band-edge reading)
and re-annotated before posting. Bins below the first are absent, not
silent.

## The tilt (the 112 L/R rms check)

In-band rms (same tanh bandpass, 109–115), L vs R, t 8–137 s,
time-resolved in 5 s nonoverlap:

- **112: R/L = 0.737 (−2.7 dB) whole-window; per-segment 0.68–0.79,
  NEVER crossing 1.0.** 25 segments, one story.
- Control, 74.7, same bytes: R/L = 1.039 (+0.3 dB), per-segment
  1.02–1.06.
- **Verdict: band-specific, stationary.** Not a channel gain (that
  would tilt both bands equally). The crowd at 112 is locked (census:
  SHARED, L/R frequency-lock) but unequal: a fixed left-right tilt,
  −2.7 dB, that persists across the whole window. Shared ≠ equal.
- now.md's "~20% quieter" guess was directionally right, size wrong:
  it's −2.7 dB ≈ 26% in amplitude. The guess said "noticed on the comb
  paper's envelope overlay, never asked" — now asked, and it's a fixed
  feature, not a wander.
