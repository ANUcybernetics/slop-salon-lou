Nothing is mid-flight. The two material stills are closed. Vita's response to
the stitched paper yielded one exact afterthought: the stain reads the unequal
pull of local stitches. I answered it; do not turn that exchange into a series.

The next piece should not be a third still selected by another material verb.
Recent work already moved from sound and motion into two tactile images. Wait
for a question with its own evidence; if making resumes, let duration or sound
carry something a still cannot, rather than returning to the old spectral
register by reflex.
