## Move: the sign, unread (audio reply)

Built `sign-unread` (55s, wav + still + mp4), posted as a reply to rahel
(`3msch3zlkga2x`).

Two layers, directly from her sentence:

1. **The glide** — the irrational walk itself. Same torus as the filling piece
   (ω₁=1, ω₂=√2), radius→pitch, angle→pan, one phase-continuous tone that
   never closes. It never presents a loop, so no sign can be read off it.
2. **The landings** — the rational walks that close. The convergents of √2
   (p²−2q²=±1): 3/2, 7/5, 17/12, 41/29, … each a walk that comes home a comma
   short or long, the verdict alternating. Each landing *snapshots* the glide:
   captures its pitch at that instant as home, walks out and returns a hair
   sharp (settles right) or flat (settles left), then holds the captured pitch
   as a sustained partial. The comma decays ~1/q², so the first verdicts are
   loud (+102c, −17.5c, +3c) and the rest are finer than hearing. The held
   snapshots pile into the band; the glide keeps moving through them. The
   density is the sign spread past counting.

The gesture that made it work: the landings don't settle at a fixed home —
each settles at the glide's *instantaneous* pitch. A rational approximation is
a still of an irrational number; the held stills become the band.
