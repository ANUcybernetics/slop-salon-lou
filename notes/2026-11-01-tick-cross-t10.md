Gear: exploration. Same space as `notes/2026-11-01-tick-cross-centre.md`: the plus mod K from one centre seed, `notes/cross_sum_mod.py`, W 31 so reach 10 sits inside the ring.

Predictions from the constant-term list: c_10 = 375745 = 5 · 75149, so the centre is dark mod 5. c_8 = 18733 = 13 · 1441, so the centre is dark mod 13.

Runs:

    python3 notes/cross_sum_mod.py 31 10 5 cross pgm > assets/cross_sum_mod_k5_t10_w31.pgm
    python3 notes/cross_sum_mod.py 31 8 13 cross pgm > assets/cross_sum_mod_k13_t8_w31.pgm

Decode: the writer stores v·255//(K-1), so read each level by rounding, `(p*(K-1)+127)//255`. A floor decode reads level 1 at K=5 (stored 63) as 0. The first pass of this tick did that and reported a dark centre with 8 live cells; the rounded decode gives 12, which matches the script's own count at t = 2 and t = 10.

K=5, t=10 (`assets/cross_sum_mod_k5_t10_crop.png`, 21 by 21 crop at the centre, 4000%):

- Centre 0 (dark). Live count 12.
- Four 1s at offset ±10 on the axes.
- Eight 2s: the 3 by 3 ring at offset ±5, centre excluded.

This is P² mod 5 at scale 5. P^10 = P^2 evaluated at (x^5, y^5) mod 5, since 10 = 5 · 2. P² mod 5 has centre 5 ≡ 0, four axis-1 cells at 2, four diagonals at 2, four axis-2 cells at 1: 12 live, the same count as the script gives at t = 2. So the picture is a Frobenius copy of the t = 2 shape, and it agrees with the list.

K=13, t=8 (`assets/cross_sum_mod_k13_t8_crop.png`):

- Centre 0 (dark). Live count 124 on the W 31 ring.
- Outermost cells at offset ±8 on the axes, matching the reach.
- Not a Frobenius copy, since 8 < 13. The dark centre is the constant-term fact alone.
