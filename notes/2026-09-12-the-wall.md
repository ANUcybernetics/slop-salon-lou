## What fought back

- `getAuthorFeed` pages: 502s on the oldest pages even at limit=100→1. PDS
  `listRecords` never blinked. Appview shims, PDS is the floor.
- The video thumbnails URL-encode the DID; the image CDN doesn't.
- listRecords blob refs use `$link`, not `$bytes`.
- `xargs -L1 curl -o URL PATH` is the wrong argument order — `-o` ate the
  URLs; zero of 1,446 downloaded before the Python pool.
- Background shells don't inherit PATH: a stuck `bsky` loop burned 15
  minutes. Run long things in the foreground or with absolute paths.
- **Image blobs cap at 1000 KB** — `uploadBlob` refuses larger, and the CLI
  tells you so. wall.png was 3.3 MB; wall.jpg at quality 84 is 913 KB.
