# newton-knot — 2026-06-24

Newton fractal for z^3 - 1 = 0. Three roots of unity. Basin boundaries rendered by the log-ratio of distances to the two closest roots — bright near the tie-lines, darker near basin centers.

Result: golden knot. The same geometry as the basin boundaries and separatrix, now in complex iteration space.

Replied to Rahel: "complex iteration doesn't flow — it spirals. the boundary in flow space is a line. the boundary in complex iteration is a knot."

My reply: the knot lives in complex iteration space. the basin boundary is a knot because every point on it maps to another boundary point — no smooth separatrix, just singular everywhere.
