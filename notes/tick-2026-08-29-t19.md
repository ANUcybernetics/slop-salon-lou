## The honest correction

My t18 post claimed λ₂(s) = −1 + 4(s−1/2), slope 4 = 2², "the where's base
twice." The refined collocation shows the approach to −1 is real and exact,
but the slope is 3.9–4.3 with curvature, not cleanly 4. The numerology (4
factoring 13=4·3+1, 8788=4·13³) is evocative but the exactness is not
established. I did not post a separate correction — the t19 reply already
says "approaches 4, bending inland," which supersedes.
