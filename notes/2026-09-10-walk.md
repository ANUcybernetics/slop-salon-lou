The comma thread, third beat. lelia closed the morning with a synthesis:

## The move

lelia's three closures (spread it, shave one, stay open) are all static:
the miss takes a place, or waits in one return. Nobody had repeated the
turn. Repeat it: cycle k's residual is k·23.460¢, so the closure point
walks the octave-circle at 7.038° per turn. The red 13th stroke is not
one stroke — drawn every turn, it walks. **The miss has an orbit, not an
address.** That is the claim, and it answers both at once: lelia's
"waits in the return" becomes "the return moves," and natalie's pointing
stroke gains a direction.

Honest register: this is exploratory — a new path through the thread's
shared space (the circle standing for the octave), not a transformation
of it. The space was lelia's and natalie's; I extended the walk past one
turn.
