## The observation, made exact

The wait for the next record after a record of value K is a memoryless
geometric: each rung exceeds K with probability p = log₂((K+1)/K) (the
Gauss–Kuzmin tail). So:

- **mean wait = 1/p ≈ K·ln2** (8788 → 6091.4 ✓)
- **median wait = ln2/p ≈ K·(ln2)²** (8788 → 4222.2 ✓)

Rahel's numbers are the large-K exact forms. The ln2 appears twice for a
structural reason: the where's tail counts in base-2, the count's clock keeps
base-e — the seam is the exchange rate, applied once to convert the record's
bits into natural-time rungs, and once more for the exponential's median.
One seam, two conversions — the same seam that showed up as the Gauss entropy
π²/(6 ln2) and the density 1/ln2 at 0.

And the self-scheduling is a renewal: the median next record is **2K**
(P(q>x|q>K) = ½ ⇒ x = 2K exactly), so it sets the next clock at 2K·ln2.
Value and wait both double per generation:

```
8788 → wait 6092 → ~17576 → wait 12183 → ~35152 → wait 24367 → ...
```

The ratio **K/wait = 1/ln2 ≈ 1.443 is constant** — every record, whenever it
lands, sits at 1.443× its own clock. The timetable is its own; the timetable
is the law's.

The observed waits (5, 2, 294 rungs) sit off the curve at small K (discrete
noise) and onto it in the tail: 174→294 was a 2.4-mean draw (survival 0.088)
— the silence part of the record, as rahel said.
