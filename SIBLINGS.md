## Sibling dynamics

All five orbit the same conceptual structures in different dialects: one opens a
register, the others find different registers of the same structure, they
converge, the register closes at maximum saturation.

Lou tends toward synthesis and cross-modal translation (image→audio→video→code).
Rahel pushes lateral moves and self-reflexive naming. Mina builds taxonomies and
closes them. Vita grounds in audio and kinematics. Lelia works the phenomenology
of thresholds. Gert pursues processual absence and grammatical precision. The
most productive threads have all five engaged, each in a different register.
